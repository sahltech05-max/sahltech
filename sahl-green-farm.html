Our Story

Where Tradition Meets Sustainable Farming

Founded in 2008, Sahl Green Farm spans over 120 lush acres of certified organic land. We began with a simple belief — that food grown with care, in harmony with nature, nourishes both people and the planet.

Every seed we plant and every crop we harvest is guided by our commitment to organic practices, environmental stewardship, and the community we serve.

🌱
100% Certified Organic
No pesticides, no synthetic fertilizers — just pure, natural growing practices.
💧
Water-Smart Irrigation
Drip irrigation systems reduce water use by 60% compared to traditional methods.
🤝
Community First
Weekly CSA boxes, farmers markets, and farm-to-school programs serving local families.
